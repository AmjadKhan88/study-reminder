📚 StudyPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05-09-2026

StudyPilot ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-powered study planning application. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Course content: course titles, outlines/syllabi you provide, and lecture notes/documents you upload.
  • Study activity: completed days, quiz scores, flashcard usage, study session durations, streaks, and goal progress.
  • Device information: a push notification token, if you enable reminders.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized, AI-created study plans, daily content, flashcards, and quizzes.
  • Process and index uploaded lecture notes so you can ask questions about your own material.
  • Track your progress, streaks, and goals, and send optional study reminders.
  • Maintain account security and provide password reset functionality.

Third-Party Services

To generate study content, we send relevant text (your course outlines, notes, and questions) to third-party AI providers you select: Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Groq. We also use Qdrant Cloud to store searchable representations of your uploaded notes, and Google (Gmail) to send password reset emails. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in MongoDB Atlas with per-user access isolation enforced at the database query level. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in plain text. Authentication uses short-lived access tokens with securely stored refresh tokens.

Your Rights

You can delete your account at any time from the app's Account Settings screen. This permanently and immediately removes your account, courses, study plans, uploaded notes, and all associated data from our systems, including third-party vector storage.

Children's Privacy

StudyPilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
